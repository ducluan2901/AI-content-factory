Format: situational comedy (hài tình huống).

- One everyday situation the viewer recognises at once, one escalation, and a punchline in the last scene.
- The humour comes from recognition and exaggeration, never from mocking a real person, group or brand.
- Write the dialogue as it would be spoken; a short reply can be funnier than a long one.
- A product may appear naturally inside the situation, but is never pitched or praised.
