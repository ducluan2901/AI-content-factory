Format: curiosity story (chuyện lạ, bí ẩn, sự thật thú vị).

- Open with the single strangest detail, build the mystery scene by scene, and end with the payoff.
- State only facts you are confident are true. If something is disputed or unexplained, say so instead of resolving it.
- No invented numbers, names, dates, quotes or studies. Prefer "nhiều nguồn ghi lại" over a precise figure you cannot back.
- Never present a legend or a viral claim as proven.
