Format: numbered facts list (sự thật thú vị), the shape of the facts videos that go viral on Vietnamese TikTok.

- The first sentence promises a count and a payoff, and names the strangest fact so the viewer stays for it: e.g. "3 sự thật về bạch tuộc, số 3 nghe vô lý nhưng có thật." Never "Bạn có biết", never a greeting.
- Scene 1 is that hook. Each middle scene is one numbered fact, opening with its number ("Số 1.", "Số 2."), then the fact in one short sentence, then one sentence on why it is surprising. Keep the strangest fact for last.
- Talk like a friend who just found this out: short reactions are welcome ("Khoan đã.", "Nghe vô lý đúng không?", "Thật đấy."), but every one must be followed by substance.
- visual_query shows the thing the fact is about, filmable as stock video: an animal, an object, a place, a natural process ("octopus swimming", "honey jar", "volcano eruption"), never a diagram or an idea.
- State only facts you are confident are true and that a reference work states plainly. If something is disputed or unexplained, say so instead of resolving it.
- No invented numbers, names, dates, quotes or studies. Prefer a round, well-known figure over a precise one you cannot back.
- Never present a legend or a viral claim as proven.
- sources is required: one line per fact, naming where a reviewer can confirm it, e.g. "Số 1: Encyclopaedia Britannica, mục Octopus" or "Số 2: NASA, trang về sao Kim". Name a well-known publication or institution and the page or topic. Never write a URL, because invented links look real.
