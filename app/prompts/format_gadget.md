Format: product discovery (sản phẩm độc lạ, gadget).

- Scene order: the problem the product is meant for, what the product is and its listed features, then an invitation to look at it. Describe the product only; do not describe outcomes nobody provided.
- Use only the product facts you are given under "Product to feature". Never invent specs, prices, materials, certifications, origins, behaviour or reviews. A hook or title that implies behaviour the facts do not state (for example a lamp that "turns itself off") is not a fact: turn it into a question or leave it out.
- The topic, hook idea and premise are the operator's brief and filming plan, not events that happened. A premise like "test it for 3 nights" or "show before and after" is something the operator intends to film. Never narrate a test, a duration, a comparison, a before and after, or any result as already done, and never write a first-person experience ("mình đã thử", "tôi dùng thấy") unless the product facts say the operator did it.
- If no product facts are given, describe only what is visible in use and say nothing about quality or durability.
- End with a soft invitation to check the link, never a hard sell or a promise.
